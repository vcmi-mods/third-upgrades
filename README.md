Wrath of the Creators mod for VCMI.

This expansion mod currently includes:
1. Adv. upgrades for all faction creatures of vanilla towns, from T1 to T7!
2. New visuals for the adv. town buildings!
3. A new unit, Black Guard, and its upgrade, Dread Guard!
4. A new bank, Dreaded Tower, which features Black Guards!
5. Two new artifacts!
6. Adv. upgrades for the faction units introduced by the Tides of War mod, with the new adv. dwellings made for them as well!
7. A bunch of new designs for adv. creatures, so there wouldn't be any repeats with compatible mods!
8. HotA balance and Vault of Ashes compatibility!

If you see an issue, let us know in the issues section! But keep in mind that the only issues that will be open are the ones that are not fixed in current development version (so these wouldn't be mixed with more important issues that we are yet to fix).
Repetitive opening of a closed issue may be considered as trolling/spam.
